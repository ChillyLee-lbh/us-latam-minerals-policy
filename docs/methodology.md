# Research Methodology

## Data Collection
The data for this research was collected from various primary and secondary sources, including government reports, academic journals, and field surveys. The research team employed both qualitative and quantitative data collection techniques to ensure a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.

## Processing
Data processing involved cleaning and structuring the data collected to ensure accuracy and relevance. Tools such as Excel and Python libraries (Pandas) were used to organize the data and prepare it for analysis.

## Analysis Methods
The analysis methods included statistical analysis, thematic analysis for qualitative data, and comparative analysis between different datasets. Statistical packages such as R and Python (SciPy, NumPy) were utilized for quantitative analyses. 

## Timeline
The research was conducted over a period of 12 months, with the following phases:
1. **Literature Review:** 0-2 months
2. **Data Collection:** 3-6 months
3. **Data Processing:** 7-8 months
4. **Data Analysis:** 9-11 months
5. **Report Writing:** 12 months

## Quality Assurance
Quality assurance measures included regular peer reviews, validation of data sources, and use of established analytical frameworks to ensure the reliability of results. Additionally, the data was cross-verified with different sources to minimize biases.

## Limitations
This research faced several limitations, including restricted access to certain data sources, potential biases in data collection methods, and limitations in the analytical techniques employed. These constraints have been addressed where possible in the analysis and results sections.

## Future Research Directions
Future research could explore broader regional comparisons and incorporate newer datasets as they become available. Additionally, examining the impact of policy changes over time may provide deeper insights into the minerals policy landscape in the region.
